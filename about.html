About Us

Triluna Company LLC is a trusted healthcare staffing agency providing hospitals, clinics, and medical facilities with highly qualified, credentialed clinical professionals nationwide. We partner with healthcare organizations to deliver dependable staffing solutions that support patient care, operational continuity, and regulatory compliance.

We specialize in placing experienced healthcare professionals across a wide range of clinical environments, helping facilities address staffing shortages, reduce burnout, and maintain consistent standards of care. Every clinician we place is thoroughly vetted and prepared to integrate seamlessly into care teams.

Guided by our mission to empower care and illuminate talent, Triluna delivers staffing solutions that prioritize quality, compliance, and compassion—supporting better outcomes for patients, providers, and healthcare organizations.

Clinician-Led Leadership

Triluna is guided by an experienced clinician-led leadership team with deep expertise in medicine, healthcare operations, and organizational management. This approach ensures our staffing solutions are informed by real-world clinical needs and aligned with the highest standards of care.
