About Us

Triluna Company LLC is a trusted healthcare staffing agency providing hospitals, clinics, and medical facilities with highly qualified, credentialed clinical professionals nationwide. We partner with healthcare organizations to deliver dependable staffing solutions that support patient care, operational continuity, and regulatory compliance.

We specialize in placing experienced healthcare professionals across a wide range of clinical environments, helping facilities address staffing shortages, reduce burnout, and maintain consistent standards of care. Our rigorous vetting and credentialing process ensures every clinician is prepared to integrate seamlessly into your care team.

Guided by our mission to empower care and illuminate talent, Triluna delivers staffing solutions that prioritize quality, compliance, and compassion—supporting better outcomes for patients, providers, and healthcare organizations alike.
