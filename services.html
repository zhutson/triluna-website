Healthcare Staffing Services

Triluna Company LLC is a physician-owned healthcare staffing company providing dependable, credentialed clinical professionals to hospitals and ambulatory surgical centers nationwide.

Hospital Staffing Solutions

We partner with hospitals to provide experienced clinicians who are prepared for complex care environments, interdisciplinary collaboration, and the operational demands of inpatient and perioperative settings.

Our hospital staffing solutions are designed to support continuity of care, patient safety, and reliable coverage during staffing shortages, surges, or planned absences.

Ambulatory Surgical Center (ASC) Coverage

Triluna specializes in ASC staffing, placing clinicians who are efficient, adaptable, and experienced in high-throughput surgical environments.

We prioritize punctuality, preparedness, and professionalism—ensuring your surgical schedules stay on track and your teams operate seamlessly.

Physician-Led Clinical Insight

As a physician-led organization, we approach healthcare staffing differently. We understand call coverage, workflow pressures, credentialing requirements, and the real-world consequences of inadequate staffing.

This clinical insight allows us to deliver staffing solutions that align with how care is actually delivered—not just how it looks on paper.
